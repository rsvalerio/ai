# Vendor Record

<!--
The fields a vendor record carries: one record per company, however many of its products
are recorded. Everything here is a property of the company, and none of it is repeated on
a product record.
-->

- **Trade name** — as the company markets itself.
- **Legal entity** — registered name and registry id, with source.
- **HQ country** — registry address country, with source.
- **Governing law** — from the dated terms or customer agreement; per region if it
  differs.
- **Parent** — ultimate parent entity and its country, or "independent", with source.
- **Jurisdiction class** — entity and parent, each named with its country.
- **Extraterritorial reach** — which law, which entity, why; or "none identified".
- **Ownership changes** — acquisitions or changes of control, with dates and classes
  before and after.
- **Funding** — only from primary announcements; otherwise omitted, not estimated.
- **Products on the map** — products recorded under the profile.
- **Recorded, out of scope** — products seen and ruled out, with the scope-map section
  that rules them out, so the decision stays visible.
- **Unknowns** — each with *Settle by: <check>*.
- **Sources** — in the evidence contract's source-record format.
